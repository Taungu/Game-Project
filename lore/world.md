# The world of Tzarot

> Lukas keeps a separate **Tzarot Lore Bible** document. Many terms here are **game-only extras** that the bible doesn't cover yet. Items, NPCs and enemies are written to tie into this lore, so new content should fit it. Names below come from item descriptions, NPC lines and design notes.

## Creation and the gods
- Before anything lived, the **deities fought for the right to shape the core's image**. **Ishdam** won. The **Father Tree** stands as proof of his triumph, won beside angels he bound so they could never rise against him (*Divine Restraints*).
- Ishdam later loved his creation more than a creator should, and what that love loosened was never bound again.
- His angels' flames are essence "as pure as it was before the world had been claimed", so fearsome that even Ishdam keeps them chained (*Angel Flames*).
- Other deities created other things. The **dragons** of the opposite end of the world are another deity's creation, older there than the Ishdarians.

## Essence (magic)
- Magic in general **is essence**. Every living being has its own version of essence, found only in living creation.
- Essence comes in **levels of purity**. The purest can only be wielded by gods, who mould it into creations.
- **Core Essence** is believed to be a second heart. Cultivated far enough, it spills through every cell until the bearer stands close to the divine (*Core Outbreak*).
- Once, essence ran through every Ishdarian as second nature. Over time it faded. The few who still carried it were called **witches**, and the Cleansers of that age all but wiped them out (*Witch's Eye*).
- **Steel of essence**: metal constantly exposed to the gift of creation and strengthened by it. A great enough forging of it is an **artifact** (*Essence Mathakot*). A weapon forged of it is a **Kleilev** (*Bavel Blank*; Bella's injector and Benesh's memorial weapon are both called Kleilev).

## The Father Tree and the Sentence of Sin
- **Da'at Rav** is the official centre of the world and holds the **Father Tree** (the "Tree of Knowledge"). In the Lobby it appears dark maroon red.
- The **Sentence of Sin** was the great corruption that made the Father Tree **ill** (the "Ill-Father"). It also killed the great ravine at the opposite end of the world.
- The ill tree's roots now creep through the land. The **Infected** are its victims and offspring: the **Deceased** (the dead, used as vessels the roots control) and the **Host** (a living person turned). The North Forest's second half is these **corrupted grounds**.
- The **Forest of Life** lies outside the North Gate. Its herbs were gathered "while the soil still remembered being rich" (*Forest of Life Salve*). *Living Roots* still carry the life the Father Tree was planted with, from before it turned ill.

## The Church, Cleansers and Opharim
- The **Church** (the Ministry) serves Ishdam. Its public bible speaks of compassion. The Cleansers' own copy is pure instruction, the formula of every prayer (*Ishdam's Scriptures*).
- **Ishdam's crest**: the poles of light embracing a tree (*Pentagon Pendant*). The **Pardian Coin** shows a piercing light through the crescent on its head and the tree of knowledge that fathers life through **Gan Pardes** on its tail.
- **Ishdam's vessel** is the **Pontif**.
- **Blessed**: a genetic trait some Ishdarians (the human race) carry that lets them push essence out of the body. On its own it does little else.
- **Cleansers**: Church soldiers who face what roots in the soil. A high Ministry rank grants a Cleanser a guardian angel, an **Opharim**. It is an invisible metaphysical being living in the **realm of purity**, where unseen things like gravity and heat exist. Opharim were made by Ishdam to serve his people and answer through him alone, bound by a pact granted by the Pontif.
- **Casting**: Cleansers memorize incantations in a language they don't understand and write letters with their fingertips. Each letter (glyph) carries a broad concept of existence. The Opharim reads them as commands and spends its host's essence to cast. Letters combine like maths into complex spells; a single letter makes the Opharim execute its raw meaning. Glyph names: **Akhar, Velith, Shoren, Tzimra, Marek, Ovin, Kesh, Ranah, Lumet, Ithav**. (*Formula of Light*: "Write it right, and something will answer.")
- **Congregants**: Church trainees drilled under Cleansers (*Congregant's Combat Wear*).
- **Cleansing's Way of Flash**: a Cleanser fighting style first walked by the **Inquisitor Lukas**. A miracle opens the way, then a single precise strike lands before the horror can turn.
- **Ziel**: had every quality of an Inquisitor but left to be a remote village priest. His flail held nothing together (*Ziel's Thrash*).
- **Wanderers**: a lowest-rank role sent onto ground left behind after a cleansing (*Iron-Willed Plate*). NPCs address the player as "wanderer".

## The Embracer, the Embracers and the Navats
- **The Embracer**: the figure the player meets in the intro, in the void after being knocked out by **Valrek**. He presents the starter Souls. The player wakes in the **"Embracing Flush"** bar (the Lobby).
- The **Navats** call him the **False Prophet**. Those who embrace him call him **the Author**. Whoever signs his contract yields half their soul to the dead and may speak with those who have passed (*Embracer's Contract*).
- **Embracers**: his followers, the usual side (PVP Team A).
- **Navats**: the opposing faction (PVP Team B). Their members are called **Seedlings**. Their line: "Eliminate those who follow the false prophet."

## The opposite end of the world
- Now a **dead desert**. Once a great ravine full of life that died in the Sentence of Sin.
- The **dragons** lived there before the Ishdarians.
- An Ishdarian legend guarded his people against the dragons for years. When the ravine died, he struck the earth with his war-hammer-like axe until it split. Water gathered in the cracks and his people lived on. That weapon is the Kleilev **Dragon Slayer**.
- **SkyShatterer**: a similar titled legendary weapon, now wielded by the opposite end's current king, "of one strike and never a second". Both weapons share an origin.

## Beasts of the North Forest
- **Lord Dohv**: the dominant Dohv. Below him in rank comes the planned **Alpha Dohv**, the pinnacle of the species. Dohv hide is prized (*Dohv-Hide Harness*: "two cubic metres of head, and the rest was this").
- **Named Bosses**: bosses that can only be met once, unlike regular bosses.
- **Valrek**: the monster that chases the player in the intro.
