# Characters of the Tzarot novels

The significant characters of Lukas's novels: Arc 1 *Graceless Life*, Arc 2 *Bordering Life*, and the side story *Inquisitor's Descent* (unfinished). These are **book** characters. The game's playable Souls are in [characters/](../characters/README.md), and the game is only partly canon. Ages are as of the main story's present (Arc 1 / Arc 2's present chapters). World and terms: [world.md](world.md).

> Spoiler policy: this repo is public, so only what the books already show is written here. Reveals Lukas is holding back are left out on purpose.

## The Vassals of Delilah (main party)

| Character | Who they are | Looks | Key facts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Klyne** (Klynehart Bar Benaiah) | Protagonist. Heir of House Benaiah, son of General Cyrus. Once Beit Elyon's prodigy knight; dropped out in his second year and spent six years as "the wet scoundrel", an Iron-Willed wanderer at Da'at Rav's North Gate. ~22. | Black hair with white strands, dark brown near-black eyes, unkempt. | His Kleilev **Hart**: a silver double-edged longsword with the split crown, ordered from Bavel at 5, given at 7. Named it Hart, "my other half" (Klyne = Klynehart without Hart). Shares his body with **Valrek**. Cyrus still names him heir. |
| **Avery** (Avira Bar Delilah) | Royal candidate of House Delilah (the house of democrats) in the **Royal Blood Accord**. Came to Da'at Rav disguised as a helmeted wanderer scout to recruit Vassals. 18. | Short dark-pink hair, deep blue eyes, slender. Steel "bucket" helmet. | Grew up poor with her father John; her mother died giving birth. Her aunt **Clara Bar Delilah** took her in, and later died. Caretaker: **Grace**. Sharp-tongued, manipulative ("I need you"), fiercely loyal. |
| **Benesh** | Chamorian, last son of the **Yosher** chieftain. His tribe was erased by the Apex Predator. 18. | ~2 m, rough dark-brown hair, sharp ears, fangs, bare chest and beast fur. His brown eyes turn blazing dark orange when his essence rises. | Fights bare-handed. Proud, earnest, slow on the uptake but perceptive. Can't hold his alcohol. Wants to keep competing with Klyne and become his friend. |
| **Mari** | Cleanser of the Church, a "Miracle curer" (a healing-focused Cleanser), stationed by choice in the neglected South Gate. Marko's twin sister. 20. | Golden hair, golden eyes, white uniform. | Righteous, a little naive, and much stronger than she looks. Wrongly blamed Klyne for Marko's death, then repented. Avery calls her a "naive hypocrite". |

## Spirits and legends

| Character | Who they are | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Valrek** | The Chamorian legend, "the behemoth", founder of the Yoshers and Arthur's only equal and close friend. | His corpse, taken by the Ill-Father Tree's roots, became the **Apex Predator** that erased his own tribe and killed Klyne. Now lives on through its core, the heart in Klyne's chest. Can read Klyne's feelings, speak to him and take over his body. Satisfied with his death; would rather not live again. |
| **Arthur Bar Benaiah** | Ancient hero, "the reflector of Ishdam's light", wielder of **Nidon Harigah**. From the Empire. | One of the five heroes who sealed the Ill-Father Tree; set the South Gate's seal. A statue of him stands there. |

## House Benaiah

| Character | Who they are | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Cyrus Bar Benaiah** | Patriarch of the Benaiah and the Empire's General of military affairs. The strongest man alive. | Orange/tangerine hair with dark roots (black in his youth), amber eyes, a split-crown burn ring around his neck. Illegitimate son of Arren and a woman from Yafo; tore his father's head off at his 18th birthday rite and took the house. Raised Klynehart with brutal "training" from age 7. Believes Klyne will return. |
| **Martha** | Klyne's mother, Cyrus's wife. | Loving and protective in his childhood. Later turned cold on him ("you're not my son", "You're just like him!"). |
| **Sandra** | Commander of the Empire, Cyrus's later spouse, Maelis's mother. | Short dark-purple hair, deep lapis eyes. Married into the failing House Izavel (her husband, its head, died); charm and skill lifted her to Cyrus's side. Arranged Maelis's engagement to Klynehart. Calls herself Klyne's "second mother"; he hates her. |
| **Dia** | Klyne's eldest sister. ~26. | |
| **Kyra** | Klyne's second elder sister. ~24. | |
| **Korina** | Klyne's youngest sister, "a child of love". ~17, soon 18. A prodigy knight in her own right. | Played knight with him as his "right hand man" when she was 3. Cyrus: "my daughter, nothing more". |
| **Sturla** | Lieutenant of the Lokhems, "Successor of Shiryon", Klyne's cousin. | Long dark curly near-black hair. Two-handed greatsword fighter. Left Shiryon at 13, returned at 16 with a Rehem's head. Klaus's answer to Cyrus's line; doesn't care for the claim but loves the rivalry, so he competes with Klyne without hating him. |
| **Klaus Bar Benaiah** | Cyrus's younger half-brother, Sturla's father. | Lost the house to Cyrus at the rite. Took branches of the family to Shiryon to rebuild Arren's legacy. |
| **Kellen** | Klaus's firstborn, Sturla's older brother. | Gentle; dreamt of a life outside the family. Sturla's return was for him. |
| **Arren Bar Benaiah** | Former patriarch, Cyrus and Klaus's father (dead). | Brought a Pendant Lila's head at his rite. Killed by Cyrus. |

## Beit Elyon (the Empire's academy)

| Character | Who they are | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Maelis Bar Izavel** | Klynehart's former fiancée and the Izavels' heir. Now a royal candidate in the Accord, a seat in the Imperial Union and the academy's main fundraiser. | Braided raisin hair in a bun, blue eyes. Top scorer, student council president. Met Klynehart as a child and swore he'd remember her name. Ran to his side in the cult attack. |
| **Erin** | Klynehart's servant from his first day at Beit Elyon; sold to the Benaiah by her father. | Brown braided hair, large eyes, timid. Slept beside him to be close during assassination attempts. |
| **Nina Rowlin** | Maelis's close friend, class 5.P. | Emerald hair. Bright, chatty, top of the class. |
| **Arwen Faelis** | Commoner scholarship student of the knight department. | Grey slicked hair, clear blue eyes. Beat Klynehart over Charlotte, then was broken by him in the duel that ended Klynehart's standing. "I see who you are." |
| **Fania** | Biology teacher and combat mentor. | Sharp features, long light-brown ponytail. Can sense core essence. |
| **Norhen Benedict** | Principal of Beit Elyon. | Large, bald, military bearing. |

## Da'at Rav

| Character | Who they are | Key facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Marko** | Silver-pristine wanderer, Mari's twin (born first). Klyne's short-lived friend. | Golden hair, fiery gold eyes, cheerful. Killed by a root-infested woman. Last wish: that Klyne look after Mari. "A man who can shed tears for someone…" |
| **Jina** | South Gate brothel worker whom Klyne saved; now a waitress. Blessed. 17. | Brown hair, amber eyes with gold flecks. In love with Klyne, calls herself "his woman". |
| **Roran Keller** | "The Baron" of the South Gate, an Empire-born self-made "entrepreneur" who ran its brothels and businesses. Core Essence duelist. (Dead.) | Slicked tan hair, beige suit, wavy rapier **Sapere**. Imprisoned and tortured Klyne. Killed by Shrolt after his fight with Klyne. |
| **Shrolt** | Roran's bandaged right-hand man, born in the South Gate. | Weak presence, yet the most dangerous man in the room. Took Roran's face and now rules the South Gate as "Roran". Brought Klyne to the cell, then freed the prisoners. |
| **Danial** | Inquisitor of the Church. | Masked, silver gauntlet, light hair with a blue tint. Credited Klyne with the Apex Predator and gave him the Pentagon insignia. Humble, apologises for the Cleansers' failures. Hidden truth: [hidden-lore.md](hidden-lore.md#danial). |
| **Pontiff Hans** | Head of the Church, who grants Cleansers their Opharim. | Prophesied with Danial to be "the gateway of paradise". Hidden truth: [hidden-lore.md](hidden-lore.md#pontiff-hans). |
