# Fahlon: "Lost Ruler"

- **Affinity:** Artillery · **Default weapon:** Bow · **Rarity:** 2★ starter
- **Role:** Ranged DPS. Marks targets as Vulnerable and punishes them. Mobile and slippery. Trade bonuses.
- **Lore:** the last prince of **Da'at Rav**, thrown out of his rite by the untold prophecy of the holy. He carved his way through the wilderness and cast aside his dignity (see *Fahlon's Carve*).
- **Affinity Stat:** none yet (deliberately undecided)
- **Essence colour:** dark blue · **Class weapon:** Fahlon's Knife (always visible)
- **Identity:** "careful with resources". His Essence pool and regen are smaller in absolute terms (100 at L1), but the refill time is the same as everyone else's.
