# Roran (in progress)

- **Affinity:** Medium **+ Light**, the **first hybrid Soul**: lore-wise he fights with a rapier, which has a Medium's reach and a Light's weight, so he can equip both. Medium is his main affinity (default weapon, icon, PVP lock). · **Default weapon:** the Medium default (StraightSword) · **Rarity:** 3★, the first **standard** 3★ Soul for Medium
- **Not a starter.** Meant for Convoke's standard pool, but **unreleased** for now: the Lobby's `ClassDefinitions` marks him `unreleased = true`, so Convoke skips him. The dev accounts get him through `GRANT_EVERYTHING`; in the Session he's on the DEV panel's Soul switch.
- **Title, lore, model:** none yet.
- **Status (2026-11-13):** Q, E and R built; the Q has Lukas's animations, the rest are filler visuals. His bare-handed Q / E and the passive aren't designed yet. No animations.
- **Identity:** a **Bleed** Soul like Luther. He's listed as a Hemorrhage contributor in `StatusEffects`, so his Physical hits feed a Hemorrhage bleed's burst.

## Playstyle and balance (2026-11-14, per Lukas)
Two forms, neither is the "peak": the R **changes the playstyle**, it doesn't power him up.

| | Normal form (bleed kit) | R form (Bare Hands) |
|---|---|---|
| Damage comes from | E True % (scales with the target's max HP), Bleed ticks, Q counters | Raw M1s, bare Q/E/F |
| M1s | **Limited: 0.6× damage** (`ClassAbilities.Roran.m1Limiter`) | Full damage (the limiter is off while `RoranBareHanded` is set) |
| Passive (dodge → Bleed) | On | **Off** |
| Feel | Passive, defensive, stable damage; rewards surviving (parries, dodges) | Aggressive brawler |

**Build paths** (how the existing scaling already lines up):
- **Pure physical**: plays around the R. Physical boosts the bare-handed M1s and skills, and every physical hit feeds Hemorrhage pools (10% of physical damage).
- **Pure tank**: plays around the parry and the dodge passive. The E's True % and the Bleed ticks don't need damage stats, so his damage stays stable while he stacks Health and resistances.
- **Mixed**: probably the strongest: tanky enough to parry and dodge, enough Physical for the R.
- **Magic**: plays purely around Bleed. Bleed ticks scale 75% off Magic and 25% off Physical (`StatusEffectManager`, `BLEED_TICK_*`), so Magic boosts the Q counter's and the passive's bleeds. Not a horrible pick, just narrow.

The M1 limiter is generic: any class can set `m1Limiter = { multiplier, offWhile }`. `CombatScript` applies it to the M1's base damage at both damage sites (before stat bonuses). It also shrinks what his M1s add to a Hemorrhage pool.

**How the parry is wired:** the stance is an i-frame window of a new kind, `PlayerInvulnerability.PARRY_KIND`. `PlayerDamageReduction.ApplyToDamage`, the shared damage choke point, blocks a hit landing in it and hands it to the handler the kit registered (`SetParryHandler`). It isn't a perfect dodge. The counter targets the nearest enemy, not necessarily the attacker, because the choke point doesn't know who hit. Damage that skips the choke point (bleed ticks, for example) isn't parried. `RoranKit` keeps real cooldowns even in Studio (`TEST_NO_COOLDOWNS = false`), because the miss cooldown is the mechanic.

**The weapon's own motion (E):** Lukas animated a stand-in part, `FakeHandle`, on `AnimationDummy`'s right arm (Session place, save "Roran E connect"), with its joint at the arm's centre like the standard grip. Its track (6 keys: the re-grip f1–68, back to the normal hold on f70) is copied as data into `ReplicatedStorage.WeaponGripMotion` (`RoranEConnect`). Every client plays it on the real weapon's `WeaponGrip` joint when the E connects (`RoranPlungeConnect`), so any weapon follows. A weapon whose grip has its own arm-side offset (BroadDagger) gets the motion re-based onto its joint (`WeaponGripMotion.Apply`), so it works without converting it. **When the stab is re-animated, the track must be re-copied.**

**E's helpers:** `StatusEffectManager.GetBleedStacks(humanoid)` and `HadHemorrhageWithin(humanoid, seconds)`. `ApplyAmplifier` now stamps a `HemorrhageAt` server-time attribute on the target when Hemorrhage lands. The True % is of **max** health, so it hits bosses hard; this is a placeholder.

**Filler look:**
- **Stance:** a pale glint over him and a widening ring of light.
- **Parry:** muted teal (116, 161, 165) **afterimages** of him trail the slide and slash (a frozen copy every 0.035 s, each fading over 0.3 s; `RoranCounterTrail`), plus a white flash, a **yellow glow round the edges of his own screen** (`Q.vfx.parryEdgeFlash`) and Lukas's **IronSpark** burst at his blade the moment the parry lands (`Q.vfx.parrySpark`), **SingleSlashVFX** on each riposte's real slash (angle and frame read off the blade tip's path: variant 1 (the one that spins him) uses variant 2's trajectory, from f29; variant 2 comes down high-left→low-right, roll −27, from f45; both sped up with the riposte; data `Q.counterAnimations[i].slash`, colour `Q.vfx.slashColor`), the blades' **CutVFX** hit effect where the slash meets them when it lands (`RoranCounterCut`; `Q.vfx.cutVfx` can name another) and a spray of blood, darker and bigger on a Hemorrhage counter.
- **Whiff:** a dull grey flicker.
- **R:** his sword is hidden and a copy of it spins away behind him, a blood-red ring of force runs out to the burst radius, and a faint red glow stays on him while it lasts.
- **Bare-handed Q:** a wide sheet of air sweeps across the hitbox with each punch, tilted left then right, with a puff of dust at his feet.
- **Bare-handed E:** the plunge's streak for the lunge, a pale flash on whoever's caught, then a burst, a white flash and a ring of force at their head for the knee.
- **E:** a red flash for the build-up, IllThorn's dash bursts / connect / impale / burst, plus his own pale streak and a spray of blood that grows with the % (a dark red flash and ring on a capped post-Hemorrhage hit).
