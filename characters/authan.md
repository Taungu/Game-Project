# Authan (personal)

- **Lukas's own character.** Granted only to his account in the Lobby; never in Convoke. Rarity "Personal" (PersonalStarBorder).
- The rig was first named "Rothan". Lukas is "nearly completely this character". When equipped, Authan's outfit is welded to the rig and Lukas's own avatar items come off except his **hair**. He keeps his own skin tone.
- **Affinity:** his own type "Authan", plus `anyWeapon = true`: he can equip **any** owned weapon. It's his dev/spec character (used to try DivineServant, IllThorn, The Commandment…).
- **Personal weapon:** **Than**, a trident staff with a 5-hit combo Lukas animated in Moon Animator (see [weapons](../weapons/README.md)).
